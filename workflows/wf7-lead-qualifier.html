Pros & Considerations

Honest Assessment

✓ Advantages

  • Qualifies leads around the clock — no after-hours missed opportunities
  • Consistent scoring — every lead measured the same way, every time
  • Natural conversation — GPT-4o handles messy, real-world answers gracefully
  • HOT leads get instant sales alerts — no time wasted on cold prospects first
  • Full CRM entry on every lead — searchable, auditable, exportable

⚠ Considerations

  • Scoring weights are fixed — adjusting criteria requires editing the workflow
