Pros & Considerations

Honest Assessment

✓ Advantages

  • Works on any device — submit from your phone at a restaurant, not from your desk two weeks later
  • Instant digital receipt — email confirmation with full details serves as proof of submission
  • Live Google Sheets ledger — finance and owners see expenses as they happen
  • Unique EXP-IDs make every expense traceable and auditable
  • No per-seat fees — unlimited staff can submit expenses
  • 8 categories map directly to common tax deduction categories

⚠ Considerations

  • No receipt image upload in base version — must attach receipts separately
  • No approval workflow built in — manager approval requires an additional n8n step
  • Google Sheets is the ledger — not a full expense management dashboard with reporting charts
  • Doesn't integrate with accounting software (QuickBooks/Xero) directly in base version
