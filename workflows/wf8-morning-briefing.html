Pros & Considerations

Honest Assessment

✓ Advantages

  • Zero friction — briefing arrives in your existing email inbox, no new app or habit required
  • Three live data sources in parallel — weather, calendar, and priorities all pulled fresh each morning
  • Fully customizable — change the priority list in Google Sheets, briefing updates next day
  • Works for teams — send to multiple recipients with one workflow
  • 365 days a year — weekends, holidays, vacations — never misses

⚠ Considerations

  • Requires Google Calendar OAuth2 — one-time setup needed
  • Priority list is manually maintained in Google Sheets — not auto-pulled from a task manager
  • Weather data is city-level — not GPS-specific for travelers
  • Static 7AM delivery — timezone adjustments require workflow edit
