Pros & Considerations

Honest Assessment

✓ Advantages

  • Instant professional delivery — client gets the proposal within seconds of submission
  • Persistent follow-up without annoying the client — spaced 3 days apart, not daily
  • Staff only escalates when automation has already tried twice
  • Unique PROP-ID on every proposal — full tracking and auditability
  • Works for any service type — consulting, accounting, agency, or trades
  • Fully logged — every proposal with timestamp in Google Sheets

⚠ Considerations

  • Proposal acceptance must be manually updated in the sheet — no e-signature integration in base version
  • Follow-up emails are templated — sales reps who want custom messages need to intervene
  • Wait nodes in n8n are time-based, not calendar-aware — won't adjust for weekends
  • No proposal PDF attachment in base version — links to the proposal document separately
