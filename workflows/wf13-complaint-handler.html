Severity Tiers

Three Tiers. Smart Routing. Human When It Counts.

Not every complaint is a crisis. This workflow identifies which ones are — and handles the rest automatically without wasting staff time.

🚨 HIGH
Legal risk, significant financial impact, or extreme sentiment score (1–3)
→ Staff escalation alert sent immediately. Draft response provided for review. Do NOT auto-send.
Human must handle this — automation identifies and escalates only
⚠️ MEDIUM
Service failure, moderate frustration, sentiment score 4–6
→ Staff receives complaint details + GPT-4o draft response. Staff reviews and sends manually.
Human in the loop — draft reduces effort, staff controls the final send
✅ LOW
Minor issue, constructive feedback, high sentiment score (7–10)
→ GPT-4o auto-sends a professional response directly to the customer. Staff notified via log only.
Full automation — appropriate for routine complaints with clear resolution paths
What GPT-4o Analyzes

Seven Dimensions of Every Complaint

One API call produces a complete complaint intelligence report — severity, sentiment, category, concerns, recommended action, and a ready-to-send draft response.

Severity

HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW — determined by risk level, financial impact, and emotional intensity

Sentiment Score

1–10 scale — 1 is furious, 10 is calm. Used to calibrate response tone automatically

Issue Category

Service quality, billing error, communication breakdown, product defect, or policy dispute

Key Concerns

Bulleted list of the 2–4 specific grievances the customer raised — extracted from natural language

Recommended Action

What should actually be done — refund, callback, policy exception, internal review, etc.

Draft Response

A full, professional response tailored to the complaint tone, severity, and issue category — ready to review or send

Pros & Considerations

Honest Assessment

✓ Advantages

  • Every complaint analyzed instantly — no queue, no "we'll get back to you in 2–3 business days"
  • HIGH severity complaints flagged immediately — legal risk never sits unnoticed in an inbox
  • GPT-4o draft reduces response time even for MEDIUM complaints — staff edits, not writes from scratch
  • LOW complaints handled fully automatically — frees staff for complex issues
  • Sentiment score gives leadership a longitudinal view of customer satisfaction trends
  • Consistent, professional tone in every response — no rep having a bad day

⚠ Considerations

  • GPT-4o may misclassify edge cases — severity boundaries need occasional review
  • Auto-send on LOW severity complaints is a deliberate design choice — review the draft response template before enabling
  • No ticketing system integration in base version — complaint history is email-based
  • Requires judgment call on which complaint types qualify as LOW severity before deployment
