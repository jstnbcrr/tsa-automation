Pros & Considerations

Honest Assessment

✓ Advantages

  • Runs every day without any human involvement
  • Three-tier escalation catches every non-responder before they fall through the cracks
  • Personalized emails — client gets their name, intake date, and service type in every message
  • Staff only gets notified when automation has already tried twice — no false alarms
  • Plugs directly into WF1 CRM — zero extra setup if WF1 is already live

⚠ Considerations

  • Relies on status being manually updated to DOCS_RECEIVED — requires staff to update the sheet when documents arrive
  • Schedule trigger won't fire during n8n Cloud downtime (rare, <0.1% monthly)
  • Reminder tone is template-based — edge cases (grieving client, language barrier) still need human judgment
  • Day counting is from clientId timestamp, not a custom intake date field — acceptable but not fully flexible
