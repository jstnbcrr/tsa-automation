What It Does

The Exact Automation Flow

From form submission to fully logged CRM entry — here's every step that runs without anyone touching a keyboard.

1

Client Submits Intake Form

Client fills out name, email, company, service interest, and notes on your hosted form. Fires a POST request to n8n in real time.

Webhook Trigger
2

Unique Client ID Generated

n8n stamps a unique ID (e.g. CLT-20260428-A7K2) and timestamps the intake date automatically.

Set Node
3

Welcome Email Sent to Client

Personalized Gmail with their name, selected service, and next steps. Branded, professional, instant — before a human even sees the submission.

Gmail
4

Staff Notification Fired

Internal email to your team with full client details: name, company, service type, contact info, and the generated client ID.

Gmail
5

CRM Row Created in Google Sheets

All fields appended to your live CRM sheet with status set to PENDING, ready for document follow-up (WF2/3) to kick in automatically.

Google Sheets
6

Error Alert on Failure

If any step fails, a dedicated error email fires to staff so nothing silently drops. Full error message and payload included.

Error Trigger

Pros & Considerations

Honest Assessment

✓ Advantages

  • Instant, personalized response — no client waits hours for acknowledgment
  • Zero human error in data capture — every field is machine-logged exactly as entered
  • Scales infinitely — 1 client or 1,000 clients, same zero manual effort
  • Live CRM in Google Sheets — no per-seat SaaS fees, shareable with your whole team
  • Automatically feeds into WF2/3 for document follow-up without any extra setup
  • Works 24/7 — a client submitting at 2am gets a welcome email at 2am

⚠ Considerations

  • Requires Gmail OAuth2 setup — one-time 10-minute configuration
  • Google Sheets is a spreadsheet, not a full CRM — limited filtering and reporting without add-ons
  • Welcome email is template-based — highly personalized long-form outreach still needs a human touch
  • If n8n Cloud goes down (rare), intake still submits but automation pauses until restored
