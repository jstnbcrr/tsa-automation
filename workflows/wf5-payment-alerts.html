This Workflow Runs Every Weekday Morning

n8n fires on a schedule every weekday at 8:00 AM. It reads your Invoices Google Sheet, calculates days until or past each due date, and routes every invoice through the correct alert tier — a friendly reminder at 3 days out, an overdue notice on day 0, and an internal escalation flag at 7+ days past due. No manual scanning, no missed invoices.

Pros & Considerations

Honest Assessment

✓ Advantages

  • Scans every invoice every weekday — no manual checking required
  • Three-tier escalation matches urgency to tone — clients aren't immediately threatened
  • Staff only escalated after 7 days — focuses human attention on real problems
  • Google Sheets as AR ledger — no per-seat accounting software license
  • Runs on a schedule — zero action required once configured
  • Each alert includes invoice ID, amount, client name, and due date — no context needed

⚠ Considerations

  • Doesn't integrate with QuickBooks or Xero directly — invoices must be manually entered in the Sheets tab
  • Alert emails go to a static address — doesn't dynamically look up account manager per client
  • No payment link in reminder emails in base version
  • Won't handle disputes or partial payments — those still need human judgment
