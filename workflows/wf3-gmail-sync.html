How It Works

What WF3 Does When Active

The full workflow logic is already built. Here's exactly what runs when the OAuth credential is restored.

Step 1 — Trigger

Gmail Inbox Watch

n8n polls Gmail every 5 minutes for unread messages from known client domains. New emails start the workflow.

Step 2 — Lookup

Client Sheet Match

The sender's email domain is matched against the active client roster in Google Sheets to pull project context and assigned staff.

Step 3 — Draft

GPT-4o Reply Draft

The email body plus client context is passed to GPT-4o, which writes a professional, context-aware draft reply in the correct tone.

Step 4 — Deliver

Gmail Draft Created

The AI-generated reply is saved as a Gmail draft — not auto-sent. Staff reviews and sends with one click when ready.
